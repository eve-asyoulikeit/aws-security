# Attacker infrastructure, but vibe-coded: tracking the evolution of credential harvesting platforms

**Source:** https://securitylabs.datadoghq.com/articles/attacker-infrastructure-but-vibe-coded/
**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Tags:** credential-abuse, bedrock, phishing-infrastructure

Datadog Security Labs examines two "vibe-coded" credential-harvesting phishing platforms, Loot and UltraVault, showing attackers now use AI-assisted development to rapidly build and iterate phishing kits. Both platforms abuse Amazon Bedrock to validate stolen credentials/secrets at scale, turning a legitimate AI inference service into a credential-checking oracle. Relevant for anyone monitoring AWS-hosted AI services for abuse patterns tied to stolen-credential validation.
